#PlayerSpanScoreDate

Every round is created and checked on the server, so scores here were earned one round at a time. Each player's best game per mode counts.

How it works

Each round shows a sequence one item at a time. Hold it in your head, then answer. Get it right and the next round is one item longer. Miss and you lose a life. Three lives per game. Correct answers in a row build a streak that multiplies your points.

The highest level you clear is your span for that mode. Most adults clear 5 to 8 digits in order and 4 to 6 in reverse. Pointers mode was made for this game and has no published norms, so compare it with your own earlier games. Scores move by about one level from day to day with sleep and focus, so watch the trend rather than a single game.
